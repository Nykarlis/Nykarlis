About these numbers

Figures are ballpark estimates, last reviewed . They come from general knowledge of typical published cost-of-travel ranges — not from a live pricing feed, and not from anyone's actual receipts.

Exchange rates are approximate and fixed in the data file, as of . They are not looked up live and will drift. Local-currency amounts are a rough conversion, nothing more.

Sanity-check anything here against real listings before you book. Prices for a single city, a single week, or a festival period can be far away from a national average.

This page runs entirely in your browser. No data is sent anywhere and nothing is saved.
